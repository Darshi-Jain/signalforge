# Renewal Recovery Skill

Goal:
Assess and recover an at-risk renewal.

Use when:
- renewal is approaching
- contraction or pricing concerns exist
- stakeholder or adoption weakness may affect renewal

Procedure:
1. Review commercial context.
2. Investigate relationship health.
3. Check adoption if value realization is unclear.
4. Check Voice of Customer for explicit objections or churn language.
5. Investigate support only when technical issues may affect renewal.
6. Recommend specific actions tied to evidence.

Completion criteria:
Return the main renewal blockers, evidence, and prioritized recovery actions.
