# Churn Investigation Skill

Goal:
Determine whether a customer is at meaningful churn or retention risk.

Use when:
- churn risk is unclear
- multiple risk domains may be involved
- the account requires a complete investigation

Procedure:
1. Retrieve customer profile and risk triage.
2. Investigate the strongest factual signals first.
3. Use only the specialists needed to resolve uncertainty.
4. Compare negative signals against positive and contradictory evidence.
5. Do not infer churn solely from one operational issue.
6. Produce:
   - overall risk
   - major risk drivers
   - positive signals
   - contradictory signals
   - missing information
   - prioritized next actions

Completion criteria:
Stop when additional specialist calls are unlikely to materially change the conclusion.
