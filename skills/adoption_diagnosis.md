# Adoption Diagnosis Skill

Goal:
Determine whether product adoption is weakening.

Procedure:
1. Review active users, seat utilization, feature adoption, and trend direction.
2. Compare with segment peers.
3. Distinguish seasonal or temporary movement from sustained decline.
4. Avoid using relationship, support, or commercial signals as adoption evidence.
5. Recommend specific adoption actions.

Completion criteria:
Return risk, evidence, missing context, and next actions.
